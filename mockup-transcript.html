Can you explain SN1 vs SN2 mechanisms?
Both are nucleophilic substitution but they work through different paths. SN1 is two-step (carbocation intermediate, slower), SN2 is one-step (concerted, backside attack). What kind of substrate are you looking at?
Tertiary alkyl halide
Tertiary substrates strongly favor SN1. The carbocation intermediate is well-stabilized by the three alkyl groups. SN2 is sterically blocked at tertiary carbons.
R₃C-X + H₂O → R₃C-OH + HX
